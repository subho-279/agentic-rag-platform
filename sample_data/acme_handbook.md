# Acme Corp Employee Handbook

## Parental leave policy
Acme employees receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave. Leave may be taken in one block or split into two periods within the first year after the child's arrival.

## Expense reports
Expense reports must be filed within 30 days of purchase. Receipts are required for any expense above 25 dollars.

## Internal project codenames
Project Falcon is Acme's unreleased logistics optimiser. Its launch is planned for the second quarter of next year and is confidential to Acme staff.
